# Exotopia Listening Booth — Audio Script Outline

*SCD Hub · exotopia.org · pon.ink*
*Draft outline — April 2026*

---

## Overview

The Listening Booth is a set of short narrated audio pieces woven into the Exotopia experience.
Each track plays at a specific moment in the user journey — arrival, transit, eco-ops mission,
cultural station, and address assignment.

Together they form an audio layer that makes the cosmic and the real feel continuous:
the same voice that describes an exoplanet 300 parsecs away also describes the water quality
work happening on Earth that earned you a place there.

**Production approach:** Minimal production. Real voices where possible. Short.
The visuals carry the scene — audio adds texture and ground, not spectacle.

## Track 1 — "First Light"

**Trigger:** Welcome page. Plays after the cinematic intro drift completes and the dome is visible.
Auto-play, fade in with scene. Skippable.

**Voice character:** Calm, gender-neutral. Slightly warm reverb — as if heard inside a pressurised
dome. Not robotic. Not breathless. Just present.

**Narrative beats:**

1. *[Opening — dome visible, soul orbs orbit]* A brief note on where you are: a real confirmed
   exoplanet, a real distance from Earth. The numbers are not invented.

2. *[Library and water in background]* The settlement here exists because someone did the work —
   eco-ops activities on Earth translated into this virtual property. Name what the work is without
   explaining it (leave the full story to Track 4).

3. *[Camera drifts toward signpost]* Orient the listener: the signpost shows what's nearby.
   The library holds what has been earned. The pyramid in the distance is the transit terminal.

4. *[Close — pyramid glow in far distance]* An invitation rather than instruction: this is yours
   to explore. Fade music bed in as narration ends.

**Tone:** Wonder, but grounded. Not a tourism ad. More like arriving somewhere you've been
told about for years and it looks exactly like you hoped.

**Production notes:**
- Beds suggested: ambient drone tuned to the host star's spectral class (cooler stars
  = warmer, lower drone; hotter stars = bright high harmonics). Can be generated per-planet.
- Reverb tail should feel like enclosed space (dome interior), not open sky.
- Allow 2-second silence before first word.

## Track 2 — "The Conduit Opens"

**Trigger:** WormholePortal animation begins. Locked to 7-second duration.
Synchronized to the four visual phases of the portal.

**Voice character:** Sparse. The voice is layered with itself at a fifth interval (no reverb, just
a harmonic ghost). Feels like the transit system itself briefly speaking. Four short utterances only.

**Narrative beats — synchronized to portal phases:**

| Time | Visual phase | Audio |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 s | E8 rings assemble | Single word or very short phrase — the name of the departure system |
| 1.0–4.0 s | Spin + radial streaks | A breath. Then: destination system name, spoken slowly. |
| 4.0–6.0 s | White flash + ring shards | Silence except for a single high tone that clips |
| 6.0–7.0 s | Emerge — arrival glow | One word: arrival cue. Warm breath. |

**Tone:** Disorientation that resolves into recognition. Clinical becoming warm.

**Production notes:**
- Do not underscore with a crescendo — the visual already handles that.
- The high tone at the flash should be clipped/distorted then cut immediately.
- Stereo field: departure name panned slightly left, destination name centre, arrival cue right.
- Could be entirely synthesised (no human voice) — worth testing both options.

## Track 3 — "The Pyramid Speaks"

**Trigger:** User clicks the pyramid at their settlement. Plays before the destination selection
panel opens. Can be skipped to go straight to destinations.

**Voice character:** Slightly deeper than Track 1. Deliberate pacing. Not ritualistic in a
mystical sense — more like a systems engineer who has read too much cosmology.
One speaker only.

**Narrative beats:**

1. *[Opening]* Acknowledge the structure: the E8 lattice is not metaphor. Eight dimensions
   of symmetry mapped onto the floor beneath you. Brief.

2. *[Routing explanation]* The conduit network follows the periphery of the great cosmic voids —
   Local Void, Boötes Void. Transit is routed through these boundaries because that is where the
   geometry thins. Keep it short and evocative, not a physics lecture.

3. *[Destination prompt]* Near systems transit direct. Further systems route through conduit
   infrastructure. Name the rough categories (under 50 pc, 50–300 pc, beyond 300 pc) as a
   navigational feel rather than precise numbers.

4. *[Close]* Invite selection. The panel opens.

**Tone:** Informed, calm confidence. The speaker has done this many times and finds the geometry
genuinely beautiful without being sentimental about it.

**Production notes:**
- Dry recording, very little reverb — this is a direct communication to the user.
- Optionally: under the voice, a very low sub-bass pulse timed to the pyramid's pulsing
  PointLight (2.2 Hz from the code). Subtle enough to feel physical.

## Track 4 — "Field Notes"

**Trigger:** Eco-ops page on first visit, or eco-ops first check-in briefing. Longer orientation
piece. Can be re-triggered from a help button.

**Voice character:** One of the community voices — ideally an actual SCD Hub participant or
facilitator. Kenyan English preferred for the core recording if community participation is
available; otherwise a proxy voice with equivalent warmth. First person.

**Narrative beats:**

1. *[Who this is for]* Not a global citizen of anywhere, but someone from a specific place doing
   specific work. Name the kind of work: mapping waste sites, testing water, tracking farm
   inputs, running a recycling point. Real activity types without the metadata labels.

2. *[The connection to the settlement]* Each check-in builds something here — a solar array goes
   up in the dome, a garden appears, the library fills. The settlement is a record of the work.

3. *[What the data becomes]* The check-in logs are open. Water quality data becomes on-chain
   certification. Field notes feed into reports that go places. The work is not consumed by
   an app and forgotten.

4. *[Invitation to participate]* Simple, direct close. The check-in form is one step. The
   first check-in assigns your cosmic address — the place in the universe that is yours.

**Tone:** Peer to peer. Not a product pitch. Not inspirational-poster energy. The speaker
believes this works because they have seen it work, and they say so plainly.

**Production notes:**
- Record in the field if possible — ambient sound of the actual work environment is a feature,
  not a problem to suppress.
- Mix: voice forward, ambient bed well under.
- If multiple languages are available (Swahili, Luo, Somali for Lamu area communities),
  record alternate language versions of this track first — they are more important than the
  English version.

## Track 5 — "OT Kulcha Signal"

**Trigger:** Cultural station button on pon.ink / pon.ink event intro. Also usable as a
station ID before a set or broadcast.

**Voice character:** MC/toaster style. OT Kulcha register. Energy, but not forced — the
greeting that comes before the music, not the hype that sells it.

**Narrative beats:**

1. *[Station ID]* Name the station / project. OT Kulcha. SCD Hub. Exotopia. Keep it short —
   this is the first 8 bars before the drop, not a sponsorship read.

2. *[Where you are]* Briefly locate the listener: in the sound space, or at an event.
   Could reference the planet / settlement in the same breath as the city / neighborhood.

3. *[Invitation]* Come through. The sound is real. The space is yours.

**Tone:** Warm, confident, culturally specific. Not aiming to be universal — aiming to be
exactly right for the community it comes from.

**Production notes:**
- Write with the OT Kulcha collaborators, not for them.
- Music bed: one of the existing Pain in the Ghetto studio recordings, or a new riddim.
- Duration is a guide not a rule — if it needs 30 seconds to land right, use 30 seconds.
- This track should feel the most alive of the set. Let it breathe.

## Track 6 — "Cosmic Address"

**Trigger:** Fired once — when the user's Exotopia address is first assigned after their
initial eco-ops check-in. Not repeatable as a standard playback (can be revisited in profile).

**Voice character:** Same voice as Track 1, but slightly more intimate. The dome is quiet.
This moment is for one person.

**Narrative beats:**

1. *[The assignment]* Your address has been assigned. Say it aloud — the coordinate string
   (e.g. `exo-surface-v1:Kepler-452b`) read as a real address, not as a code. It is a real
   address. The planet exists.

2. *[What the planet is]* Three or four real facts about the host star and planet: distance,
   type of star, something about the planetary conditions. Not a textbook entry — two
   sentences at most. Keep the wonder without the lecture.

3. *[What you've claimed]* This address is permanent and public. It is on-chain. It is
   yours because of work you did, not money you spent.

4. *[Close]* The settlement dome is now rendered for this location. Welcome home.

**Tone:** Unhurried. Private. The opposite of a fanfare. The address matters because the
person receiving it matters — not because the technology is impressive.

**Production notes:**
- The address readout in beat 1 should be the *actual* address string, dynamically inserted
  by the app at playback. The surrounding narration is fixed; the address text is a
  placeholder in the script filled at runtime.
- At the close, a brief musical phrase — three or four notes, live instrument if possible.
  Not a sting. Something that sounds like a door quietly opening.
