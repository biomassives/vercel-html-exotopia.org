## 1. Who this covers

These guidelines apply anywhere you can post, comment, or otherwise interact
with other members on Exotopia — blog comments, the private member-to-member
comment feature, community submissions, and citizen-science data you choose
to share publicly. They supplement, and don't replace, `legal-terms.md` § 6.

## 2. The basics

- Be honest about who you are and what you're sharing. Don't impersonate
  another person, organization, or the Exotopia team.
- Don't harass, threaten, or target another member, on or off the platform.
- Don't post content that's unlawful, infringing, or that violates someone
  else's privacy or data protection rights.
- Don't use the Service to solicit or facilitate financial scams, phishing,
  or unauthorized collection of other members' wallet or payment
  information.

## 3. Location, presence, and finding other members

Exotopia's map and navigation surfaces — including the DefenderNav strip you use to move
between systems and settlements — never show you another member's live location,
position, or movement; see `SPEC_DEFENDERNAV.md` §1.6. This is enforced as a product
design constraint, not only a conduct rule.

- Don't use any feature of the Service, or information gathered through it, to locate,
  track, or attempt to determine another member's real-world or in-app position without
  their explicit consent.
- If you want to coordinate or meet up with another member, that's between the two of you
  on a platform of your choosing — Exotopia doesn't build in-app tools for finding a
  specific other person's location, and isn't the right place to ask for one.

## 4. The private comment feature specifically

Exotopia's member-to-member comment feature is built around small, mutual
"green-light" connections (capped at seven per thread) rather than public or
proactively-monitored messaging — see `legal-privacy.md` for the full privacy
model. Two things follow from that design:

- **We don't watch your comments** — there's no automated content scanning
  or moderator review of a comment unless it's reported.
- **You can report a comment you receive.** This is the mechanism that keeps
  a low-surveillance design workable — if someone sends you something that
  violates these guidelines, use the report action; a moderator will review
  the reported content. We do not act on unreported content in this feature.
- **Composing a comment requires self-attesting as 18 or older** during
  onboarding (see `legal-privacy.md` § 9 and `legal-terms.md` § 2 on
  eligibility). This is a self-attestation, not a verified check — we don't
  collect a birthdate or ID.

## 5. Citizen-science and field-data submissions

If you submit PFAS, environmental, or other citizen-science data:

- Submitted values are **community-submitted, not certified laboratory
  results** — treat them, and present them to others, that way. Don't
  characterize submitted data as a certified test result or a medical/safety
  determination.
- If you're reporting a named site or brand, be accurate — named entities can
  dispute a report through the correction-request path on that feature, and
  bad-faith or knowingly false reports may be removed.

## 6. NFT/collectible content

Collector cards, settlement deeds, and other minted items on Exotopia are
digital collectibles, not investments (see `legal-terms.md` § 4). Don't post
content — in comments, listings, or elsewhere — that frames them as having
guaranteed or expected resale value, or that solicits buying/selling outside
whatever mechanism the Service itself provides (currently: none — Exotopia
does not operate a secondary marketplace).

## 7. Enforcement

We may remove content, restrict features, or suspend an account that
violates these guidelines, proportionate to the severity and — where
practical — with notice. Repeated or severe violations (harassment, scams,
impersonation) can result in permanent removal.

## 8. Reporting a problem

- **Within a specific feature** (e.g. a private comment): use that feature's
  own report action, described in § 4.
- **Anything else**: use the "Report a problem" link in the site footer, or
  file an issue at the project's public repository.

## 9. Changes to these guidelines

We may update these guidelines from time to time; material changes will be
noted with an updated date at the top of this document, consistent with the
changelog practice described in `legal-terms.md` and `legal-privacy.md`.
