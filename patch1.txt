Subject: [PATCH 1/2] fix: remove ranked rarity tiering from GalaxyPage;
 correct stale revenue-split line in README

GalaxyPage.vue rendered LEGENDARY/RARE/UNCOMMON/COMMON tiers with
"extreme rarity" copy on the planet panel, which links directly to /mint.
RISK_REDUCTION_RECOMMENDATIONS.md 1 identifies ranked scarcity tiering on a
pre-mint surface as the clearest Howey-test signal available to a regulator,
and MintPage.vue already asserts "no rarity" - the two surfaces contradicted
each other.

planetRarity() becomes planetClass(): same physical thresholds, descriptive
labels, no ordered tiers, no scarcity language, colours keyed to
temperature/composition rather than a value ladder.

README listed "80% of settlement revenue to settlement owner" as a stable
assumption. That described the secondary-sale aftermarket removed in the
risk-reduction pass (blog-risk-reduction-pass.md). Actual shipped behaviour is
a mint-time resonance split with seller_fee_basis_points = 0 and no secondary
market. Corrected, and made the no-passive-yield position explicit.
